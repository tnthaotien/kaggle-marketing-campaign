# MARKETING CAMPAIGN

Analyze customer dataset from marketing campaign to identify the key factors influencing customers’ decisions to participate

# Insight
- The most responsive group: **Millennials (30–55 years)** with **medium–high income (70K–105K)** and **higher education**.  
- **Single or not in a relationship** customers tend to respond better.  
- **Long-term customers with recent purchases** and **higher total spending** are more likely to engage.  
- **Wine** is the most purchased category, and **store + web** are the top-performing channels.  
- Customers who have joined previous campaigns show **higher acceptance rates**, emphasizing the value of **retargeting loyal customers**.
